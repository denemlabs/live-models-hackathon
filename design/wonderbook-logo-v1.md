## Final generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: final raster logo concept for WonderBook, an interactive bedtime storybook web app for American children and their parents.
Primary request: Create one beautifully crafted, simple brand logo with an open magical book symbol and the exact wordmark "WonderBook". Easy to recognize, read, and remember. Warm, imaginative, trustworthy, contemporary children's publishing sensibility, charming without being babyish.
Style/medium: crisp flat vector-style graphic rendered as a high-resolution PNG, clean confident shapes, no textures or mockups.
Symbol: an elegant open book whose two spreading pages subtly suggest a W, with a single small four-point magical star rising from the central fold. Strong compact silhouette, balanced negative space, avoid tiny decorative details.
Composition: one horizontal logo lockup, book symbol beside the wordmark, optically balanced with generous breathing room. The wordmark is dominant and reads clearly at a website header size. Friendly soft rounded serif lettering, carefully kerned, custom editorial feel. Capital W and B, all other letters lowercase. Spell W-o-n-d-e-r-B-o-o-k exactly.
Color palette: deep forest-green wordmark and book with one warm golden star accent, suitable for the project's warm cream and muted sage storybook interface.
Background: genuinely transparent background with alpha, no painted checkerboard.
Constraints: render "WonderBook" exactly once. No tagline, no additional labels, no variations board, no extra characters, no gradients, no shadows, no 3D, no border, no watermark. Original graphic concept, do not reproduce an existing brand logo. One finished logo only.
